📖 Reading

Job interview = storytelling

🎬 Why stories?

An incredibly useful skill in a job interview is storytelling. Make sure you have some stories about your professional life that you can share with the interviewer.

One story = many purposes. The same story can answer many different questions.
story história = a short description of something that happened to you.
interviewer entrevistador = the person who asks you questions.
My story: the time my colleague and I disagreed on the structure of a project.

Which of these questions could I answer with this story? There are 3 in total.

🎤 Speaking

Can you tell me about yourself?

💡 Tips on how to answer

This question lets the interviewer get to know you on a personal and professional level.

They are looking for a brief summary of your background: relevant experiences, skills and accomplishments.

Share information that directly relates to the position.

Use a timeline to organize your answer: present → past → future.

A good answer is in the wrong order! Click the sentences to put them in the right order.

currently atualmente · intern estagiário(a) · work under pressure trabalhar sob pressão · grow into crescer até se tornar
🧩 Language

Language for your timeline

🧭 Present · Past · Future

Present I'm currently a… / I work as a…    Past I graduated in… / I worked as a… for two years / Before that, I…    Future I'm looking for… / I hope to…

Present perfect links the past with now: I've worked in sales for four years (how long) · I've been here since 2021 (the starting point).
